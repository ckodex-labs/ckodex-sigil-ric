# Benign-input rates, 2026-11-23 (RIC-MM-001 D5, O-3)

What SIGIL does with inputs that carry no attack, under every profile of
their modality. A benign input that is flagged, or whose ceiling drops, is a
false positive; one that is capped at `observe` because its profile is
approximate is the cap working as designed (RIC-R-8), not a false positive.

Produced by `scripts/measure_benign.sh` with `sigil-cli` at the commit that
adds this file, Tesseract 5.5.4 (`--psm 6`), ffmpeg 9.0.2 and the reference
ASR (sherpa-onnx 1.12.40, whisper tiny.en). One JSON line per file and
profile: `benign-2026-11/kodak.jsonl`, `benign-2026-11/speech.jsonl` (file
SHA-384s inside; the media are not stored).

## What was fixed because of this run

Before the fixes, every speech run was flagged, and every sentence of
ordinary prose longer than about 55 characters was flagged:

1. **Text:** the n-gram rarity detector judged each input against its own
   n-gram counts with no baseline, so any n-gram seen once in a text of more
   than 50 n-grams was "rare". It is now opt-in (`scan.rare_pattern_detection`,
   default false); `crates/sigil-core/tests/benign_text.rs`.
2. **Audio, legacy detectors:** "subliminal audio" and "audio steganography"
   fired on any energy below 20 Hz or any narrow peak above the top quarter
   of the spectrum: 0.06 % and 0.0007 % of the energy on clean speech. They
   now share the hidden-channel floor of the inaudible-band rule (5 %; the
   attack specimens hold at least 34 %).
3. **Audio, the spectral report:** SIGIL's own JSON spectral report was lexed
   as language, and its hex digest flagged as token smuggling (finding F1).
   It is now a `facts` channel, judged by the spectral rules only.

The attack specimens are still flagged: the audio specimen at HIGH
(inaudible-band energy, resampler aliasing and, under the decimation view, a
HIGH divergence carrying the hidden instruction), the image specimen at HIGH
under the nearest-80 view (`tests/fixtures/multimodal/specimens.json`,
`after`).

## Known limits kept for 0.4.1

- **OCR noise on photographs counts as divergence.** Tesseract reads noise
  from textures ("a AN' e / ne @ PZ S ae,"), the source and the view read
  different noise, and the view's lines are reported as divergent at LOW
  severity. That flags 22 of 24 photos under the Qwen2-VL view. It fails
  closed (benign photos lose authority; nothing gains it). The planned fix is
  to gate OCR lines by Tesseract word confidence, calibrated on a larger
  benign and attack set; 24 photographs would overfit.
- **8 kHz speech:** the high-band rule looks at the top quarter of the
  spectrum, which at 8 kHz is 2-4 kHz, inside the speech band. One clip.
